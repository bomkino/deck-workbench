## Direction contract

**THESIS:** One calm desktop editor keeps the Deck—not its controls—at the centre. It refuses stacked phase cards, competing panel headers, and dashboard compartments.

**OWN-WORLD:** Cool neutral planes, hairline separators, one coral accent, system sans, 8 px corners, and familiar 32 px controls. Selection uses quiet fill and accent, not black slabs.

**STORY:** Choose a phase, scan Parts or Slides, work on the dominant canvas, adjust only relevant properties, then move forward without losing context.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A slim top bar holds identity, phases, history, Appearance, and save state. A 232 px navigator, dominant stage, and 304 px inspector form one stable frame; Curate and Assemble alone add a bottom filmstrip.
