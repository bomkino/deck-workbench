# Product

Workbench is a Mac-only prototype and designer-handoff tool, not a final-production design application. Final copy plus image decisions and notes become rough slide direction and a portable folder for designers. Defaults: 2576 x 1080, full bleed, readable provisional text, a useful gradient. Curate and Assemble are working surfaces; import and export are actions. See docs/MAC_APP.md and docs/KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md.
